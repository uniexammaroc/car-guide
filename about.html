About Car Guide

Car Guide is an independent vehicle information website created to help readers explore cars, motorcycles, trucks and buses. Our goal is to make basic vehicle research easier by bringing model information, specifications, reference photos and general maintenance guidance together in one place.

What you can find here

  • Vehicle makes, models and model-year browsing.
  • General specifications and reference information.
  • Commonly reported issues and basic maintenance guidance.
  • Links to external sources for further research.

Editorial and accuracy note

Vehicle specifications, availability, equipment and known issues can vary by country, model year, engine and trim. Some information may be incomplete or estimated. Always verify important details with the vehicle manufacturer, official records or a qualified mechanic before making a purchase or repair decision.

Car Guide is an independent information project and is not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer.
