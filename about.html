About Car Guide

Car Guide is an independent informational website designed to help visitors explore cars, motorcycles, trucks and buses. Our vehicle pages bring together model information, reference photographs, common issues, maintenance pointers and links to relevant public resources.

Our goal is to make vehicle research easier to understand. We aim to explain technical topics in clear language and encourage readers to verify important details before making a purchase or repair decision.

How we prepare information

Vehicle specifications and model availability can vary by country, trim, engine and model year. Some vehicle lists and recall information are retrieved from public databases, while general summaries and photographs may link to Wikipedia or Wikimedia Commons. Always check the original source and the vehicle's official documentation.

Important note

Car Guide is an educational reference, not a vehicle inspection service, repair workshop, seller or manufacturer. Prices and common-problem summaries are indicative and may not apply to every vehicle. Consult a qualified mechanic and check a vehicle's history before buying.
