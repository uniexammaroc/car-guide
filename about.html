About Car Guide

Car Guide is an Arabic and English guide to cars, trucks, motorcycles and buses. For each model it brings together photos, engine and power data, common problems with practical fixes, and official defect (recall) reports.

Where the information comes from

Brand and model lists and recall reports come from the U.S. NHTSA public databases. Photos and short descriptions come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, each under its own license. Engine, power, price ranges and the problem and fix notes for the featured models are compiled by our team from general automotive knowledge.

Please note

Prices and specifications are approximate and vary by version, year and market. NHTSA covers vehicles sold in the United States, so some models sold elsewhere appear only in our own list. This site does not replace a mechanic's inspection.
