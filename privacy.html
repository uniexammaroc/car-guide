Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Car Guide ("we", "the site") handles when you visit this website.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal data through forms. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and operation. Your browser may store your language choice locally on your device (localStorage). It is not sent to us.

Third-party services

To show vehicle lists, photos and official recall data, your browser connects directly to the U.S. NHTSA (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and api.nhtsa.gov) and to Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (wikipedia.org and commons.wikimedia.org). These services receive technical information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Advertising and cookies

We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before using advertising cookies.

Children

The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Information accuracy

Content on this site is general information only. Prices, specifications and problem descriptions are approximate. Always consult a qualified mechanic and official sources before repairs or purchases.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
