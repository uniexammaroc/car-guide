Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Car Guide (“we”, “our” or “the site”) handles information when you visit this website.

Information we collect

Car Guide does not currently ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal details to browse vehicle information. If you contact us through GitHub, the information you choose to include will be handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website or third-party services used on it may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, security, analytics or advertising. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or advertising.

Google AdSense and advertising

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense in the future. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services by visiting Google's partner sites policy. Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. The availability of controls may depend on the user's region and account settings.

Third-party services and links

Vehicle information, images, recall data and external links may be provided by or point to third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their data practices.

Data security and children's privacy

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information. This website is a general-audience information resource and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website or its services change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through Car Guide's GitHub Issues page.
